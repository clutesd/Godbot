# Simulation-era documentary camera

For an Arrival run's first 60 months, the Historian gives ordinary human activity priority over
wide context. In addition to its recurring representatives it considers one living witness of
each current activity in each camp, so building, gathering, movement, social life and rest can
appear without granting anyone simulation importance. Recent editorial memory discourages
repetition; watching a person repeatedly no longer permanently exhausts their candidacy.
Explicit event focus continues to take precedence.

Early camp sequences start on the Historian's subject and contain at most three shots within
12 world units. They do not require a wide establishment or aerial release. A rare wider
Historian anchor is presented as one shot rather than expanded into a scenery sequence.
Camp approaches frame a resident rather than the empty center, and travelers have closer,
lower framing. Existing action-aware compositions, subject visibility checks, meaningful
holds and physical acquisition remain authoritative.

Scenic flights are eligible only after six documentary shots and only when the Historian
has selected ordinary landscape context. They never replace a human, event or named landmark
story. Terrain and wildlife subjects must be within 28 world units of the acquired scene;
when no local subject is suitable there is no forced remote excursion. Early Historian context
also searches nearby rivers, valleys and landmarks. Scenic presentation stays silent and
never makes presentation wildlife into authoritative simulation history.

Manual mode retains exclusive control. On release, the visible camera pose is unchanged and
velocities are reset before physical recovery. Interrupted flights retain their unseen destination.
Fresh destinations choose a cinematic azimuth independently of the manual angle, then use the
existing sightline search and jerk-limited flight. Springs, flight limits and simulation clocks
are unchanged.

Verification covers early selection balance at months 1, 24 and 60, deterministic selection,
unchanged simulation state, compact local planning, nearby wildlife selection, manual authority
and recovery, physical flight frame-rate consistency, forest sightlines and visibility corridors.
Live visual review is still required to judge the rendered edit across terrain seeds.
