# Hybrid architectural art and validation

The production workshop hybrid lives on `feat/hybrid-workshop-facades`, unmerged. This continuation completes Claude's unfinished layered SVG pass and fixes the integration defects exposed by rendered close-ups. The next refinement pass corrects roof units and proportions, restores missing gable infill, assigns roof joinery its actual construction material, and removes coarse or duplicate projections. The later glazing pass extends window artwork to glass-capable houses, civic halls, markets, workshops and factories. Further art direction and roof surface artwork remain outstanding.

## Production integration

`BuildingSpec` still resolves the building from the authoritative development response. `FacadePlan` now shares the four opening layouts, structural post/beam rhythm, cultural band levels, material assignments, reveal depth, and measured roof batch references with `BuildingComposer`. Frame members and band levels are no longer independently recalculated by the SVG author. Existing `GeometryBuilder` batches, construction piece metadata, shadows and LOD remain in use. Architectural decisions remain spec/composer-owned. The separate transport fix below reconciles canonical inventory before construction budgeting; it does not add an architectural or inventory authority.

The full-wall admission rule covers closed, single-mass stone/ceramic workshop cores. A separate window-only admission uses the spec?s real glass material and resolved facade opening style; its projection is restricted to a single planned window per triangle and leaves the surrounding concrete, metal, glass wall skin and historic fabric intact. Timber sheds, annexes, mixed-generation fabric and dedicated compositions retain their existing surfaces. The composer recesses eligible fitted doors and panes behind the wall face, ahead of their opaque backings; this corrects the black panel that formerly covered the upper door. Cultural bands are authored into the SVG for admitted cores instead of retaining duplicate projecting pattern geometry. Roof coverings retain procedural shading. Spec pitch now produces rise over half the clear span instead of full building width, and specified eave projection is applied as a physical distance along both axes. The workshop fixture's main roof rise falls from approximately 0.984 to 0.405 canonical units (59%). Legacy shell pitch keeps its original interpretation. Layered roof rises shrink with their own footprint, and crown monitors, course lips and exposed flues are less oversized.

Closed gable roofs have real end infill and bargeboards. The canvas accepts an explicit spec material role for roof members and still merges batches by resolved material, avoiding stone rafters when the wall frame is masonry and roof structure is timber. Roof-stage joinery retains its own procedural material rather than receiving wall artwork. Buried frame members are slimmer; solid masonry supports use stepped corbels. Eligible masonry wall construction units meet flush, preserving construction piece boundaries while removing coarse grooves competing with SVG joints. Resolved workshop yard walls use connected low sections with a front entrance instead of detached stone bollards. The legacy exterior forge heat marker is suppressed on resolved specs because their equipment already emits its actual hearth.

## Artwork improvements

The seeded SVG composition produces matched albedo, relief and roughness atlases. It includes irregular stone outlines, geology variation, pinning spalls, pocks and fractures; tooled dressed stone, chamfers and mason marks; interlocking quoin lifts; carved cultural string courses and relieving heads above rectangular openings; oak boards, grain, knots, iron straps, clout nails and a ring pull; individually tinted leaded quarries and saddle bars; putlog holes, lichen, damp, soot and sill runoff.

This continuation adds distinct ashlar and brick bonds instead of applying rubble to every material, retains grain and pegging on actual timber frames, uses resolved sash divisions for later windows, and varies weathering with climate and maintenance. Ornament follows the supplied fictional culture grammar; historical/cultural accuracy has not been independently certified.

The GPU now consumes all three matched maps. Colour uses sRGB texture decoding; height and roughness remain linear data. Relief perturbs the surface normal under real scene lighting, with a restrained cavity contribution to indirect diffuse light. Original occlusion hooks remain active; procedural grain relief is suppressed on painted faces so it does not compete with the artwork.

All elevations share one physical texel density and padded atlas packing. Projection uses the same layout as SVG composition. Triangles crossing the facade boundary are excluded as whole triangles, fixing stretched texture stripes on caps, returns and projecting equipment. Interior and roof faces retain their production materials.

## Early-modern, industrial and modern glazing

The browser previously supplied no glassmaking capability for its default/stone-rich evidence. The resolver correctly chose opaque fittings even in later periods, so those cards did not attest glass. `glazed-masonry` now provides an explicit representative glass-capable toolkit from early modern onward; `composite` includes the capability needed for ordinary glass as well as curtain glass. These are browser comparison inputs, not changes to simulation capabilities or the resolver. Actual world buildings only gain glass artwork when their authoritative spec resolves real glass. Full-wall artwork also respects opaque timber/paper fittings instead of painting leaded glass onto them.

Window-only SVG coverage now includes the five glass-capable building functions, including inherited walls. A shared `glazingFrameWidth` keeps physical casings, muntins and painted pane edges coherent. Divisions come from `spec.openings.divisions`; the art does not create extra rows. Individually tinted sheets have putty fillets, slim factory frames, insulating-unit spacers, restrained deposits and occasional linen blinds. Later rectangular openings no longer receive an unconditional medieval arch or putlog decoration. Physical culture bands and their backing stop at clear openings; their shallow later relief avoids the large gold blocks that previously crossed the glass.

The modern market?s scaled precinct path now publishes its `FacadePlan` through the same helper as ordinary shells, using the exact grammar that emitted its geometry. Construction stage ownership, simulation authority, LOD distances and historic massing stay intact. Window-only admission starts at fitout. The full-wall workshop path continues to show its unfinished walls earlier.

All real glass materials receive a view-dependent Fresnel reflection approximation from the scene?s hemisphere light, with real directional highlights retained. It follows weather and night without extra render targets. Painted panes can use lower roughness than masonry. Cached shader clones read the live source emissive colour and intensity: Three pre-multiplies its built-in emissive uniform by the clone intensity, so merely updating a second intensity uniform was insufficient. The Architecture Browser now drives the production palette?s night factor and a cool night environment as well as dimming the sun. Night snapshots therefore exercise actual window illumination.

## Performance and limits

The owner cache admits four variants with two concurrent raster jobs. Each variant uses three 1024-square RGBA textures: approximately 16 MiB with mipmaps, 64 MiB at capacity. Additional independent seeds fall back to procedural materials. Active textures are not evicted from scene clones. Stage variants share artwork; resources dispose with their asset builder. Full-detail painted batches add 12 bytes per vertex; lower LODs do not carry facade attributes.

Benchmarks use headless Edge/ANGLE on an RTX 4060, 15 warm renders and 90 synchronized samples per run, alternating original/hybrid materials. Both modes retain identical calls/triangles within each scene. Single-building timings approach timer resolution. Repeated street/crowd runs vary, including first hybrid street samples slower than subsequent samples; they do not support a reliable universal percentage cost or speedup. In the roof refinement run the ten-building street had 164 calls / 171,408 triangles; its first hybrid median was 1.9 ms and its second was 0.3 ms, against original-material medians of 0.5 and 0.3 ms. The 64-clone hybrid medians were both approximately 1.4 ms against original-material medians of 1.6 and 1.4 ms (873 calls / 737,558 triangles). Runtime and GL errors were zero across 78 recorded orbit/stage/variant cases.

The geometry refinement itself increases the single-fixture render from 18 calls / 15,386 triangles to 21 calls / 16,610 triangles. Correct material roles and additional physical surfaces have a real cost; the SVG toggle still changes neither calls nor topology within the revised asset. Crowded measurements include other renderer work and are noisy. These are desktop observations, not a low-end-device budget.

The remaining visual limits are the procedural roof-covering pattern, limited gable surface art, repeating close-range ornament and some coarse incidental equipment. Surface normals do not create displaced silhouette detail. Roof SVG artwork, low-end GPU validation and broader cache coverage remain outstanding. The browser street fixture uses production assets; the separate seed replay now confirms a running simulation also renders, but it is not a comprehensive live-settlement performance benchmark.

Earlier full-suite runs had simulation failures reproduced at starting commit `2f241502`. This pass addresses the photographed transportation initialization exception and does not claim to resolve all other simulation failures. Build warnings for large chunks/dynamic imports remain. Release approval remains outstanding.

Next: develop roof/gable surface artwork and close-range ornament, extend facade planning to annex generations, resolve the remaining society route expectation and other recorded simulation failures, and profile lower-memory hardware. Unrelated camera changes in the workspace are preserved. Nothing was merged into `main`.


## Roof and massing profile pass

The production composer now renders resolved flat/flat-parapet roofs as horizontal decks with continuous perimeter walls and thin metal coping. Gables have planar coverings, closed end walls, correctly pitched bargeboards, gutters and restrained ridge caps. Shed roofs have closed triangular side walls and a raised rear wall. Sawtooth roof rise is calculated over each individual bay, with vertical north-light glazing, mullions and valley/ridge flashing; the former full-building-span teeth and ornamental front shell are removed.

Secondary wing, twin and court masses retain their existing production footprints and historical materials, but now have matching foundations and subordinate roofs. Modern flat-roof entrances receive planar canopies. Roof curvature follows the resolved form and period; engineering roofs no longer inherit cultural upturns. Traditional curvature stays bounded by the actual rise and overhang. Rafter ends and brackets sit below the roof rather than puncturing it, and thin covering-material ridge caps replace large timber ridge blocks. Sacred and civic ridge finials are smaller and seated on physical bases.

`BuildingSpec` now reserves stacked layered crowns for shrine, civic-hall and gatehouse functions. Domestic and work buildings retain their cultural roof language with a single tier. This decision is made once in the authoritative spec resolver, with no new simulation, material-availability or economic authority. Modern forms continue to follow the spec's archetype. The shared FacadePlan records the resulting production roof batches; no separate SVG roof or massing system was added.

Both production material-library programs and legacy roof surfaces now align slate/tile courses and standing seams to each slope instead of bending them around a polar arc. Roof plate courses alternate joints and use restrained physical relief. These are shared material shader changes, with no additional raster maps, reflection maps or roof texture cache.

The capture harness now places overview cameras far enough away to inspect the complete massing. Earlier overview images made at 0.95 canonical units could crop or enter the roof; use elevation captures for historical comparisons. Final evidence and measured performance are recorded below. Render captures are a finite set of viewpoints, rather than a guarantee for every possible camera position. Lower LOD roofs, detailed annex elevations, handmade gable/roof SVG art, richer interior spaces and low-end hardware remain further work. Existing generic props, frame repetition and coarse masonry still keep some assets below the requested world-class target.
