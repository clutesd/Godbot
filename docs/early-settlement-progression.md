# Early settlement progression

Founding camps reconsider ordinary development every three months during the first five years. Each camp has a reproducible planning season derived from its identity. Emergency establishment still responds monthly to local food, shelter, materials and weather pressures; buildings and discoveries are not granted by the calendar.

Ordinary construction reserves a site for one month before paid assembly starts. Small open sites require at least three work months, ordinary buildings four, and higher levels add two work months per level. Temporary shelters can advance by at most 45% per month and permanent establishment huts by 20%. These are ceilings: actual labour, delivered materials, capabilities, sponsors and weather can slow or stop work. A stalled project cannot bank elapsed time or spend another work allocation when evaluated again in the same month.

Construction starts publish a history event with the purpose, site, material bill and required labour. Completion records elapsed construction months. Existing construction presentation follows the paid progress, and service remains tied to the authoritative structure state.

Settlements with development state no longer receive generic era or specialization scenery. Those legacy paths invented drying racks, wells, saw benches, mines and kilns independently of construction. Physical structure, resource and cargo renderers own the simulated settlement's objects.

Validation: `tests/early-construction-pacing.test.ts` exercises staged payment, completion, duplicate evaluations and independent planning seasons. The founding establishment suite checks the first five years on three seeds, empty-stock survival, resource collection and shelter coverage. These checks do not replace a visual playthrough of the opening.
